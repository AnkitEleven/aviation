# NIMS School of Aviation

Landing page and interactive web portal for the NIMS School of Aviation, NIMS University Rajasthan.

## Features
- **Programmes**: Undergraduate, Postgraduate, and Diploma programmes in Aviation Management and Cabin Crew.
- **World-Class Infrastructure**: Cockpit simulators, flight decks, aircraft training zone, engine labs, and passenger service reception.
- **Top Recruiters & Placements**: Placements with SpiceJet, IndiGo, Air India, Vistara, AIASL, Bout Technologies, and Nexus DMC.
- **Eminent Faculty**: Detailed interactive profiles for academic mentors and aviation educators.
- **Industry Exposure**: Practical training and educational visits to travel agencies (Mayur Travels) and airports.
